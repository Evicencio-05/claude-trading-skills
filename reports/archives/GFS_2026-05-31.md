## Phase 2 — Business & Products

### 2a. Business model

Contract semiconductor manufacturing — GF fabs wafers for fabless and IDM customers, monetizing via wafer starts, mask sets, and design-enablement (PDKs/IP). End markets: automotive (~35% mix historically), smart mobile, IoT, comms infrastructure, aerospace/defense, data center.

### 2b. Products & technology

Specialty nodes: FD-SOI, RF-SOI (majority of smartphone FEM modules), silicon photonics (AMF acquisition), BCD for power. R&D focused on differentiated analog/power/RF, not sub-7nm logic. Investor Day 2026: path to **40% gross margins by 2028** and **$1B silicon photonics run rate exiting 2028**.

### 2c. TAM & growth runway

Global foundry TAM ~$170B+ and growing with AI; GF's served market (≥10nm specialty) can nearly double by 2030s per management. 3–5 year runway if LTAs hold and photonics/automotive recover.
