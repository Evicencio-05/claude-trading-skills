## 2. Business & Products

**Business model.** ADI sells analog and mixed-signal ICs, software, and subsystems via direct sales and distributors. Revenue is hardware-driven with long product lifecycles in industrial and auto; pricing power improves with utilization.

**Products & technology.** Core franchises: data converters, amplifiers, RF/microwave, MEMS sensors, power management, and DSP. Q2 FY26 mix: **Industrial 50%** ($1.80B, +56% YoY), **Auto 24%** (+2%), **Communications 15%** (+79%, AI data center power), **Consumer 11%** (+23%). R&D ~$509M in Q2 (~14% of revenue). **Empower Semiconductor** acquisition (announced May 2026, ~$1.5B cash) adds integrated voltage regulators and silicon capacitors for AI vertical power — meaningful revenue expected FY27.

**TAM & runway.** Analog TAM grows with electrification, industrial automation, and AI infrastructure power density. ADI is mid-cycle recovery after 2023–24 inventory correction; management cites multiyear industrial and data-center buildouts.
