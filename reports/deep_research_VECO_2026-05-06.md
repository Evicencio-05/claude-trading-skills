## 2. Fundamental Analysis

### 2a. Company Overview

Veeco Instruments designs and manufactures precision process equipment for semiconductor, compound semiconductor, data storage, and advanced packaging markets. Unlike giants Applied Materials and Lam Research, Veeco deliberately occupies high-value niches where it faces limited direct competition.

**Key Revenue Segments (FY2025):**

| Segment | Revenue Share | Key Products |
|---|---|---|
| Semiconductor | ~72% ($477M) | LSA (Laser Spike Annealing), IBD (Ion Beam Deposition), Wet Processing |
| Compound Semiconductor | ~15% | MOCVD, MBE for GaN, SiC, InP |
| Data Storage | ~10% | HDD recording head tools (cyclical) |
| Scientific/Other | ~3% | Research instruments |

**Crown Jewels:**
1. **EUV Mask Blank IBD** — Virtual monopoly in supplying ion beam deposition tools for EUV photomask blanks. Every leading-edge chip at TSMC, Samsung, and Intel uses EUV, and Veeco's IBD is a mandatory step.
2. **InP Laser Manufacturing** — Now the biggest growth driver: Lumina MOCVD + Spector IBD + WaferEtch wet processing for 800G/1.6T optical transceiver production.
3. **Laser Spike Annealing (LSA)** — Leading tool for sub-5nm transistor annealing; sole supplier to several major logic fabs.

**Growth Drivers (next 3 years):**
1. Silicon photonics / InP laser build-out for AI data centers (~$2B opportunity over several years; $700M SAM by 2030 for this segment alone)
2. Advanced packaging capacity expansion (more than doubled YoY in Q1 2026)
3. EUV mask blank demand tied to AI chip demand
4. Post-merger scale and cross-sell with Axcelis (ion implant tools)

**Biggest Risks:**
1. China export control escalation (28nm/40nm LSA now effectively restricted)
2. Chinese antitrust blocking the Axcelis merger
3. H2 revenue concentration (back-half loaded guidance has execution risk)
