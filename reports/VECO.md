## 2. Fundamental Analysis

### 2a. Company Overview

Veeco Instruments designs and manufactures advanced process equipment for semiconductor, compound semiconductor, data storage, and photonics applications. The company generates revenue primarily from capital equipment sales (~85%) and a recurring services/parts stream (~15%).

**Revenue Segments:**

- **Semiconductor** (~74% of revenue): LSA systems (HBM/DRAM), IBD systems (EUV mask blanks, silicon photonics), wet wafer processing (advanced packaging), lithography systems
- **Compound Semiconductor** (~20%): MOCVD systems (InP, GaN), MBE systems
- **Data Storage / Other** (~6%): Legacy lapping, dicing, PVD (profitable, declining)

**Competitive Moat:**

- Near-monopoly in IBD for EUV mask blanks — no production-scale alternative exists
- Leading MOCVD for compound semiconductors (InP lasers, GaN power)
- LSA gaining tool-of-record wins against Applied Materials in advanced memory

**Top 3 Growth Drivers:**

1. Silicon photonics/InP laser volume for 800G/1.6T AI data center transceivers ($250M+ backlog secured)
2. HBM memory expansion: LSA at production tool-of-record at leading HBM maker; evaluation win at second Tier-1 DRAM maker
3. EUV mask blank IBD: secular growth as EUV adoption accelerates

**Top 3 Risks:**

1. China SAMR regulatory approval required for Axcelis merger; also export license risk on LSA (one $8M system blocked in Q1)
2. Gross margin recovery pace (compressed to 36% GAAP in Q1 2026; targeting 40%+ non-GAAP)
3. Cyclical WFE downturn sensitivity (FY2025 revenue -7.4% YoY)
