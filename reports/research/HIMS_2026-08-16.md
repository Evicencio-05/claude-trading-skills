## 2. Business & Products

**2a. Business model.** Hims & Hers sells subscription-based telehealth consults plus fulfilled Rx and OTC products via web/app (cash-pay DTC). Monetization is recurring billing at defined cadences; customers buy for speed, discretion, and bundled care vs traditional clinics. US still dominates (~$622M / 83% of Q2); Rest of World scaled via Eucalyptus (~$131M Q2).

**2b. Products & technology.** Core verticals: weight loss (GLP-1 branded pivot since Mar 2026), sexual health, hair care, skincare, wellness, diagnostics/lab, and emerging peptides/anti-aging. Q2 subscribers 2.89M (+19% YoY); monthly revenue per avg subscriber $92 (+21% YoY). Gross margin fell to 64% (from 76% YoY) as branded obesity drugs replaced higher-margin compounded mix. R&D/tech spend elevated (T&D $55M in Q2). Product cycle: mid — weight-loss scale-up + international build-out; compounding era largely behind them.

**2c. TAM & runway.** US cash-pay obesity + men’s/women’s specialty telehealth remains multi-year; management targets ≥$6.5B revenue / $1.3B Adj. EBITDA by 2030. Runway depends on branded GLP-1 economics, retention, and international density — not TAM scarcity.
