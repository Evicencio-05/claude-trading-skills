## 2. Fundamental Analysis

### 2a. Company Overview

**Business Model:**
Everspin Technologies is the world's only publicly traded, vertically integrated MRAM company.
It designs, manufactures (at its Chandler, AZ fab and soon at Microchip's Oregon fab), and
sells MRAM products directly to OEMs and through distribution.

**Revenue Segments:**

1. **MRAM Product Sales** (~90% of revenue): Toggle MRAM and STT-MRAM chips
2. **Licensing, Royalties & Foundry** (~10%): IP licensing and process services

**Products:**

- Toggle MRAM: Mature technology, defense-qualified, radiation-hardened variants
- STT-MRAM: Next-gen, higher density; 128Mb xSPI qualified, 256Mb in qualification
- TMR Sensors: Magnetic field sensors for industrial applications

**End Markets:**

- Industrial Automation & Factory Automation
- Automotive / Transportation
- Aerospace & Defense (41% of MRAM market revenue — highest segment)
- Data Centers (power-loss protection)
- Medical Equipment

**Competitive Moat:**

- Only pure-play publicly traded MRAM company
- 238 active design wins as of 2025 (vs. 178 in 2024) — sticky customer base
- Proprietary MRAM manufacturing process (IP retained in Microchip deal)
- Defense-grade qualifications (MIL-SPEC, radiation-hardened) create high switching costs
- ITAR compliance adds barriers to foreign competition

**TAM:**
Global MRAM market projected to grow from ~$4.5B (2026) to $58B+ by 2035 at 32.8% CAGR,
driven by STT-MRAM adoption in embedded applications. Defense/aerospace remains the
highest-revenue segment at 41% market share.

**Top 2-3 Growth Drivers:**

1. U.S. defense onshoring (CHIPS Act + Navy RDT&E programs; $40M contract is proof-of-concept)
2. STT-MRAM density ramp (256Mb qualification H2 2026, enabling data center and new markets)
3. Microchip foundry partnership doubling manufacturing capacity by H2 2027

**Top 2-3 Risks:**

1. No consistent GAAP profitability (lost $0.6M in 2025; sporadic earnings)
2. Customer concentration and inventory cycle exposure (Japan customers destocked in 2024)
3. Execution risk on Microchip fab ramp (first products not until H2 2027)
