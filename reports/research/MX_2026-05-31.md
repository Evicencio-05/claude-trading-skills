## Phase 2 — Business & Products

### 2a. Business model

Magnachip designs and manufactures analog/mixed-signal **power semiconductors** — primarily discrete MOSFETs/IGBTs (Power Analog Solutions, ~90% of revenue) and power management ICs (PMICs, DC-DC converters). Revenue comes from standard-product sales to OEMs, ODMs, and distributors across consumer, computing, automotive, and industrial end markets. Customers buy for cost-competitive, application-specific power efficiency in space-constrained designs (smartphone battery protection, server PSUs, EV inverters). The company exited its display driver IC (DDIC) business and is now a **pure-play power semi** with in-house fabrication at its Gumi, South Korea fab.

### 2b. Products & technology

**Product lines:**
- **Power Analog Solutions (PAS):** MV/LV MOSFETs, Super Junction, IGBTs (650V/1200V for solar/industrial), BatteryFETs for smartphone PCM
- **Power IC (PIC):** AC-DC/DC-DC converters, LED drivers, PMICs — consolidated under MSK subsidiary Jan 2025

**Q1 2026 product launches:** 8th-gen 12V BatteryFET, 8th-gen 40V/60V MV MOSFETs for AI servers/HPC, 24V BatteryFET for tri-fold smartphones (mass production, supplying major global OEM).

**R&D:** $6.7M in Q1 2026 = **14.5% of revenue** — elevated for a company still operating at a loss. Targeting **55 new-generation products in 2026** (vs. 4 in 2024, 55 in 2025).

**Product cycle:** Early-to-mid cycle on new-gen portfolio, but **legacy products still represent the vast majority of revenue** in 2026 per CEO Martino. Qualification-to-revenue lag is 6–18 months.

**Moat elements:** SSCFET® proprietary technology, 45-year process expertise, ~1,000 patents, in-house fab (vertical integration).

### 2c. TAM & growth runway

Global power discrete market ~$30–35B (Omdia/industry estimates, 2025). Magnachip TTM revenue ~$179M = **<1% penetration**. Automotive power semi TAM growing ~14% CAGR (2024–2027, Omdia). AI server power demand is a new TAM wedge but MX is a **small challenger** vs. Infineon, onsemi, and TI.

At flat ~$180–200M revenue run-rate, meaningful growth requires new-product mix shift — management guides this as **gradual through 2026–2027**. Plausible growth runway: 3–5 years IF 55-product rollout converts to design wins at scale.

## Phase 3 — Competitive Intelligence

### 3a. Market position

Magnachip is a **niche challenger** with low-single-digit share in power discretes globally. Share has been **stable to slightly declining** on legacy ASP pressure in China, offset by new design wins in smartphones and automotive. Not a market leader in any major segment.

### 3b. Competitor comparison

| Company | Ticker | Market Share | Revenue (TTM) | Gross Margin | Key Advantage vs MX |
|---------|--------|-------------|---------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Magnachip | MX | <1% power discretes | ~$179M | 15.6% (Q1'26) | Baseline — niche fab + BatteryFET IP |
| Infineon | IFNNY | ~19% power semi | ~$16B | ~40% | Scale, automotive dominance, SiC/GaN |
| onsemi | ON | ~8% power semi | ~$7B | ~45% | SiC leadership, auto/industrial breadth |
| STMicroelectronics | STM | ~7% power semi | ~$13B | ~47% | Integrated IDM, auto + industrial |
| Texas Instruments | TXN | ~15% analog | ~$16B | ~58% | Broad catalog, fab scale, margins |

Magnachip **wins** on customized BatteryFET/MV MOSFET designs for Korean/Japanese smartphone OEMs and Hyundai Mobis EV programs. It **loses** on price in legacy consumer/industrial MOSFETs vs. Chinese entrants and on breadth vs. TI/Infineon. The competitive gap is **narrowing on legacy, potentially widening on new-gen** if design wins scale — unproven at revenue level.

### 3c. Moat assessment

**Type:** Intangible assets (patents, process IP) + partial cost advantage (in-house fab) + switching costs (qualification cycles).

**Assessment:** **Stable but thin.** Fab ownership helps margin control but Gumi fab electrical infrastructure upgrades pose disruption risk. Patent portfolio is real but insufficient vs. IDM scale. Moat is **not widening** until new products reach >30% of revenue.

### 3d. Primary competitive threat

**Chinese power semi entrants** undercutting legacy MOSFET ASPs — CEO explicitly cited China pricing pressure as the primary gross margin headwind (Q1 GM 15.6% vs. 20.4% in Q2 2025). This is actively eroding the ~85% of revenue still tied to legacy products in 2026.
