## 2. Business & Products

### 2a. Business Model

Robinhood earns revenue from **payment for order flow (PFOF)**, **net interest income** on cash and margin balances, **Robinhood Gold subscriptions** ($5/mo premium tier), **crypto trading spreads**, and emerging **prediction markets / banking / credit card** interchange. Customers are predominantly US retail investors aged 18–40 seeking zero-commission access to equities, options, and crypto.

### 2b. Products & Technology

- **Trading:** Equities, options, fractional shares, 24-hour market access
- **Robinhood Gold:** Margin, research, higher yields — subscribers +37% YoY (Q1)
- **Crypto:** Self-custody wallet, tokenized equities roadmap
- **Banking/Credit:** Robinhood Cash, Gold Card, new **Platinum Card** (late 2026)
- **AI Agents (May 2026):** Beta agents for automated stock trading and credit card purchases
- **Trump Accounts:** Custodial savings app with BNY Mellon (launched app stores May 2026)
- **Prediction Markets:** Event contracts expansion (regulatory-sensitive)

R&D focus: AI/agentic UX, international expansion (UK), tokenization. Product cycle: **early-to-mid** on AI agents; **mid** on core brokerage.

### 2c. TAM & Growth Runway

US retail brokerage TAM is mature but **wallet share** is expanding via banking, credit, and crypto. Crypto + prediction markets add optionality. Management targets continued double-digit revenue growth through subscription and interest income mix shift.
