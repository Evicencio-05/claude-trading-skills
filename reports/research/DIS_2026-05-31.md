## Phase 3 — Competitive Intelligence

### 3a. Market position

**Streaming:** #2–3 globally by subs (behind Netflix); **leading bundle** with Hulu/ESPN. **Parks:** Dominant US destination share; international expanding (Shanghai, Paris expansions).

### 3b. Competitor comparison

| Company | Ticker | Streaming Share | Key Advantage vs DIS |
|---------|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Disney** | **DIS** | Top 3 SVOD | IP + parks + sports rights |
| Netflix | NFLX | #1 premium SVOD | Scale, ad tier, low churn |
| Warner Bros Discovery | WBD | #3–4 | HBO IP; weaker parks |
| YouTube | GOOGL | Ad-supported video | Gen Z time-share |

Disney **wins** on IP depth, parks, and sports (ESPN). Disney **loses** on engagement growth vs YouTube/TikTok and on subscriber scale vs Netflix.

### 3c. Moat assessment

**Type:** Intangible assets (IP, brand) + switching costs (annual passes, bundle habit). **Trend:** **Strengthening** in streaming margins; **stable** in parks; **eroding** in linear TV.

### 3d. Primary competitive threat

**Time-share competition from YouTube/TikTok** for younger demos, plus **Netflix ad-tier scale** in streaming — both pressure engagement growth without destroying parks economics.
