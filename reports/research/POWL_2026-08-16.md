## 2. Business & Products

**2a. Business model.** Powell sells engineered-to-order electrical distribution and control systems — integrated power control rooms (PCR/E-houses), arc-resistant switchgear, MV breakers, MCCs, bus duct — typically via direct sales and EPCs on project contracts (mostly % of completion). Customers buy for application engineering, factory packaging, and commissioning on complex industrial sites rather than catalog gear alone.

**2b. Products & technology.** Flagship is the packaged PCR / electrical house integrating third-party LV breakers (often from Eaton/Schneider) into Powell-designed MV systems (480 V–38 kV). FY25 mix: oil & gas 37%, electric utility 25%, commercial & other industrial 16%, petrochemical 14%, light rail 4%, other 4%. Product cycle: mid — capacity expansions (Jacintoport yard by FY26 close; leased Houston/Ohio capacity; greenfield under evaluation) to digest mega data-center and LNG awards. Moat is process/engineering know-how and project execution, not a patent wall.

**2c. TAM & runway.** Electrification of industry, grid upgrades, LNG export, and AI data-center power are multi-year demand pools; Powell is a niche specialist (tiny share of broad capital-goods) with years of work already booked ($2.4B backlog, ~$1.3B expected in 12 months). Runway is capacity and execution limited more than TAM limited.
