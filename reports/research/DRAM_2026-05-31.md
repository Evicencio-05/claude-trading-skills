## Phase 4 — Ecosystem & Structural Risks

### 4a. Korea timezone gap (~49% of fund)

Roughly **half the portfolio** is Korean-listed (Samsung + SK Hynix). KOSPI closes **~3:00 a.m. ET**. Korean stocks can move **14 hours before** US ETF reopens — DRAM cannot arbitrage intraday. During volatility, **NAV can gap** at US open vs. prior close implied value.

### 4b. Swap / derivative layer

Prospectus risks: **counterparty credit**, **valuation mismatch**, **illiquidity in OTC swaps**, magnified losses in corrections. Roundhill uses swaps primarily for **RIC compliance**, not speculative leverage — but economic exposure is still derivative-linked for part of the book.

### 4c. Flow risk (crowding)

**$12.8B AUM from zero in ~8 weeks** creates:
- Forced buying on inflows (helped the rally)
- **Reflexive risk on outflows** — redemptions force selling into a thin memory group
- Record-fast AUM growth often coincides with **short-term tops** in thematic ETFs

### 4d. Tax considerations

Standard ETF tax treatment (IRA: tax-deferred). Korean withholding on dividends generally handled at fund level; swap dividend-equivalent payments have favorable recent Korean tax tribunal precedent for legitimate TRS structures (not tax advice).
