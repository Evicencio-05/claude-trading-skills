## Phase 2 — Business & Products

### 2a. Business model

AST SpaceMobile generates revenue from **MNO partnerships** (capacity prepayments, revenue share), **government contracts**, and **hardware/service delivery** as the BlueBird constellation deploys. Pre-commercial phase transitioning to revenue ramp in 2026.

### 2b. Products & technology

**BlueBird satellites** — large aperture antennas enabling 4G/5G broadband from space to standard phones. Block 2 satellites expected to **double peak data speeds** vs Block 1. **~3,000+ patent claims** per company filings.

R&D >100% of revenue (pre-scale). Product cycle: **early commercial** — FCC authorized; first meaningful revenue Q1 2026.
