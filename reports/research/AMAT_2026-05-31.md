## 3. Competitive Intelligence

### 3a. Market position

**#1 by total WFE revenue** (~$30B+ annualized run-rate). Leader in **deposition (~40%)**, **CMP (~35%)**, **ion implant (~45%)**. Not leader in **lithography (ASML)** or **etch (Lam ~50%)** or **process control (KLA ~55%)**.

### 3b. Competitor comparison

| Company | Ticker | Market Share (niche) | Revenue scale | Gross Margin | Key Advantage vs AMAT |
|---------|--------|---------------------|---------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Applied Materials | AMAT | Breadth leader | ~$32B FY26E | ~50% | Baseline — platform breadth |
| Lam Research | LRCX | Etch ~50% | ~$18B | ~48% | NAND/HBM etch intensity |
| KLA Corp | KLAC | Process control ~55% | ~$12B | ~60%+ | Inspection monopoly, higher margins |
| ASML | ASML | EUV 100% | ~$35B | High | Lithography bottleneck |
| Tokyo Electron | TOELY | Dep/etch | ~$20B | ~45% | Share gains in Japan/Korea |

AMAT **wins** on integrated deposition/CMP roadmaps at leading nodes. It **loses** in optical/eBeam inspection share to KLA and etch intensity to Lam. Third-party 2025 data suggests **SAM share erosion** — monitor Q3 commentary.

### 3c. Moat

**Switching costs + intangible process IP.** Installed base of tools across global fabs makes replacement costly. Moat **stable in core deposition/CMP**, **eroding at margin in inspection** per industry analysts.

### 3d. Primary threat

**KLA/Lam taking incremental WFE dollars** in AI-era inspection and etch, plus **China revenue normalization** if export rules tighten.
