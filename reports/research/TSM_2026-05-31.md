## 2. Business & Products

### 2a. Business Model

Pure-play **contract foundry** — manufactures chips designed by fabless customers. Revenue from wafer fabrication (leading-edge nodes) and advanced packaging (CoWoS).

### 2b. Technology Nodes

- **N3/N3E:** Current leading edge — AI accelerators, mobile
- **N2:** HVM Q4 2025; **fully booked through 2026**
- **CoWoS:** Advanced packaging for Nvidia/AMD AI GPUs — supply constrained
- **Geographic diversification:** Arizona, Kumamoto (Japan) fabs ramping

### 2c. TAM

Global foundry TAM growing **>15% CAGR** driven by AI. TSMC share **72%+** and widening at leading edge.
